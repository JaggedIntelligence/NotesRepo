### There is unlimited demand for intelligence.

https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2020201006123135103

>Unlimited demand for intelligence at near-zero marginal cost means intelligence stops being the scarce input.
>
>Taste, judgment, and the ability to ask the right question become the bottleneck.
>
>The returns flow to people who can direct intelligence, not people who provide it.


>That’s the real trade: the value of raw intelligence is cratering while the value of knowing what to do with intelligence has never been higher. And that gap is only getting wider.
